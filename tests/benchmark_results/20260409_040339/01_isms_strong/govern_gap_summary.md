# Govern Function — Executive Gap Summary
*(CIS MS-ISAC NIST CSF Policy Template Guide 2024)*

## Executive Summary

The Govern function is rated as Substantiially Implemented across 17 in-scope subcategories. While no subcategories were left unaddressed, significant gaps exist in documentation and formalization. Critical gaps include the lack of documented mission statement alignment (GV.OC-01), stakeholder requirement mapping (GV.OC-02), and ownership assignment for regulatory requirements (GV.OC-03). Key recommendations involve integrating RACI matrices into job descriptions, documenting resource requirements per role, and establishing formal review processes for vendor contracts.

## Critical Gaps

1. GV.OC-01: Must document the mission statement, map assets to it, and ensure annual leadership review of alignment.
2. GV.OC-02: Must formally map specific stakeholder cybersecurity requirements to policies.
3. GV.OC-03: Must assign ownership for Legal/Regulatory Requirements Register entries and establish an annual review cadence.

## Key Recommendations

1. Ensure RACI matrix roles are included in job descriptions and onboarding for GV.RR-02.
2. Document specific resource requirements per role/control and track allocation annually for GV.RR-03.
3. Define cybersecurity responsibilities in vendor contracts and assign internal owners for GV.SC-02.
4. Develop a review process for new vendor contracts to include breach notification timelines for GV.SC-05.
