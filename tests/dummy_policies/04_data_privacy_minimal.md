# Data Privacy Policy
## Organization: Corner Store Retail LLC
## Version: 1.0 | Date: 2022

---

## 1. Introduction

Corner Store Retail LLC collects customer information during sales transactions. We understand the importance of protecting this information and have put basic safeguards in place. We comply with applicable consumer protection and privacy laws.

---

## 2. What Data We Collect

We collect the following customer information:
- Name and contact information
- Payment card information (processed through our POS system)
- Purchase history for loyalty program members
- Email addresses for newsletter subscribers

---

## 3. How We Use Customer Data

Customer data is used only for:
- Processing transactions
- Loyalty program management
- Sending promotional emails (only with consent)
- Legal compliance

We do not sell customer data to third parties.

---

## 4. Employee Access to Customer Data

Only employees who need customer data to do their jobs may access it. Employees must log in with their individual username and password. Employees must not share their login credentials.

---

## 5. Password Rules

All employee accounts must be password-protected. Passwords should be at least 8 characters. Do not write your password down or share it with others.

---

## 6. Data Storage

Customer data is stored in our point-of-sale system and loyalty program database. Paper forms with customer information should be stored securely and shredded when no longer needed.

---

## 7. When Things Go Wrong

If an employee discovers that customer data may have been accessed without authorization, they should immediately report it to the store manager. The manager will notify the owner, who will determine what steps to take. We will comply with any notification requirements under applicable state law.

---

## 8. Employee Rules

All employees must:
- Protect customer information
- Log out of computers when stepping away
- Not access customer data for personal reasons
- Report any suspected data misuse to management

---
