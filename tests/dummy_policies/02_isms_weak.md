# Information Security Policy
## Organization: Bright Future Nonprofit
## Version: 1.0 | Effective Date: March 2023

---

## 1. Purpose

This policy establishes basic information security guidelines for Bright Future Nonprofit staff. We are committed to protecting the data entrusted to us by our donors, volunteers, and program participants. Our mission is to serve our community, and we must protect the data that supports that mission.

---

## 2. Who This Policy Applies To

This policy applies to all staff members, volunteers, and any contractors working with our systems or data.

---

## 3. Password Requirements

All staff must use strong passwords:
- Minimum 8 characters
- Must include at least one number
- Do not share your password with anyone
- Change your password every 90 days

Our IT Manager is responsible for enforcing these password rules.

---

## 4. Email and Internet Use

Staff should only use organizational email for work purposes. Be cautious of suspicious emails. Do not click on links from unknown senders. Internet use on organization computers should be for work-related purposes only.

---

## 5. Data Handling

Donor information and program participant data is confidential. Do not share this information outside the organization without authorization from your manager. Store files on the organization's shared drive, not on personal computers or USB drives.

---

## 6. Lost or Stolen Devices

If you lose a work device or suspect it has been stolen, report it to the IT Manager immediately. This includes laptops, phones, and tablets.

---

## 7. Software Installation

Do not install unauthorized software on organization computers. Contact the IT Manager for any software you need for your work.

---

## 8. Reporting Security Problems

If you notice something suspicious on your computer or the network, contact the IT Manager right away. We take security concerns seriously.

---

## 9. Training

New staff will receive basic security training during orientation. We encourage staff to complete online security awareness courses when available.

---

## 10. Enforcement

Staff who violate this policy may face disciplinary action.

---

*Last Updated: March 2023 | Approved by: Executive Director*
