# Gap Hunter

## Goal

We are making a policy engine where we take policy that is aligned with any of ISO , NIST or any other Industry Frameworks and do gap analysis on them according to **CIS MS-ISAC NIST Cybersecurity Framework Policy Template Guide (2024)**, identify the gaps and generate a revised policy in the format of **NIST-framework policy template format**.

## Dev environment tips

- Always use **uv** package manager and `uv add package_name` or `uv remove package_name` for adding or removing any package.
