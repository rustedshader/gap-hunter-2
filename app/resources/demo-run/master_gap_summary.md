# NIST CSF Gap Analysis — Master Executive Summary
*(CIS MS-ISAC NIST CSF Policy Template Guide 2024)*

## Executive Summary

The organization faces significant security control gaps across the governance, identification, and protection domains, as evidenced by zero addressed subcategories across the entire scope. Critical deficiencies exist in establishing foundational policies, risk management frameworks, and data protection mandates. Specifically, the most critical gaps involve establishing organizational context, defining risk management objectives, and implementing comprehensive supply chain risk management. To mitigate these risks, immediate action must focus on developing and implementing core policies for asset management, access control, and mandatory data encryption for both data-at-rest and data-in-transit. We must prioritize establishing formal policies for risk appetite, stakeholder communication, and supply chain due diligence across the enterprise. Remediation priorities should focus on immediate policy development, followed by establishing foundational controls, and finally, implementing comprehensive operational procedures.

## Top Recommendations

1. Develop and update the security policy to explicitly address legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements, risk appetite, and stakeholder expectations.
2. Implement a formal Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management (C-SCRM) program, including pre-engagement due diligence and supplier offboarding procedures.
3. Immediately mandate encryption for all data-at-rest locations and establish robust key management controls to resolve data protection gaps.
4. Develop formal policies for Asset Management, Access Control, and Identification/Authentication to establish foundational security controls.
5. Establish comprehensive policies for System and Communications Protection and Incident Response to address all out-of-scope requirements.

## Remediation Priorities

1. Immediate: Mandate data-at-rest encryption and establish key management controls (PR.DS-01, PR.DS-02).
2. Short-term: Develop foundational policies for Asset Management, Access Control, and Identification/Authentication (ID.AM-1, ID.AM-2).
3. Medium-term: Establish the overarching Governance framework, including risk appetite statements and formal Supply Chain Risk Management processes (GV.RM-02, GV.SC-01).
