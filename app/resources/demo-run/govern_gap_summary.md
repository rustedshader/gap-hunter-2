# Govern Function — Executive Gap Summary
*(CIS MS-ISAC NIST CSF Policy Template Guide 2024)*

## Executive Summary

The assessment of the Govern function reveals significant gaps, as zero of the 19 in-scope subcategories were addressed by the current policy. Nineteen subcategories were found to be 'Not Addressed,' indicating a complete lack of documented controls across the governance domain. Critical gaps exist in areas such as establishing organizational context (GV.OC-01), defining risk management objectives (GV.RM-01), and managing legal/regulatory requirements (GV.OC-03). Key recommendations involve developing a comprehensive security policy that explicitly incorporates risk appetite, stakeholder expectations, and formal supply chain risk management processes to address these deficiencies.

## Key Recommendations

1. Develop and update the security policy to explicitly address legal, regulatory, and contractual requirements (GV.OC-03).
2. Establish formal risk appetite and tolerance statements within the policy to guide risk response decisions (GV.RM-02).
3. Implement a formal Cybersecurity Supply Chain Risk Management (C-SCRM) program, including pre-engagement due diligence and supplier offboarding procedures (GV.SC-01, GV.SC-05, GV.SC-06, GV.SC-10).
4. Document risk management strategies and communication channels for reporting and escalation (GV.RM-04, GV.RM-05).
5. Incorporate requirements for stakeholder analysis and communication into policy development (GV.OC-02).
